# advanced Sales analysis Project

> **Turning raw customer and sales data into actionable business insights using SQL Server.**

## 🚀 What Is This Project About?

How do you find **which products are driving revenue, which customers are most valuable, and how sales are changing over time**?

This project answers those questions by analyzing customer, product, and sales data using **SQL Server**.

Instead of looking at raw tables, I transformed the data into meaningful business insights using SQL techniques commonly used by Data Analysts.

---

## 🎯 Business Questions I Answered

🔹 How are sales changing over time?

🔹 Which products generate the highest sales?

🔹 Which product categories contribute the most to total revenue?

🔹 Who are the most valuable customers?

🔹 How can customers be segmented based on their purchasing behavior?

🔹 How can product performance be compared with historical performance?

🔹 What percentage of total sales comes from each product/category?

🔹 What are the cumulative sales trends?

## 📈 Analysis Covered

### 📅 Change-Over-Time Analysis

Analyzed sales across different time periods to identify trends and changes in performance.

### 📊 Cumulative Analysis

Used window functions to calculate cumulative sales and understand sales progression over time.

### 🏆 Performance Analysis

Compared product performance to identify high-performing and underperforming products.

### 🧩 Part-to-Whole Analysis

Measured how individual products and categories contribute to overall sales.

### 👥 Customer Segmentation

Grouped customers based on purchasing behavior and spending patterns.

### 📦 Product Segmentation

Segmented products based on their sales performance.

### 👤 Customer Report

Created a customer-level report containing important sales and customer metrics.
